About us

Big enough to get the job done, small enough to care.

Titanium Air was founded in 2014 by Colin Gilmour and is based at Lanseria International Airport. The company is built around comfort, ease and personal attention for every guest.

From light aircraft for bush flying to jets for business and long-range travel, we match the aircraft to the trip, whether you are travelling alone or with a full group.

Quality & safety

Safety comes before everything.

Regulated operations

Titanium Air operates under the oversight of the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA), within the Civil Aviation Regulations and Civil Aviation Technical Standards, and applicable sections of the Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993.

Safety & quality system

An integrated safety and quality management system covers pre-flight preparation, aircraft maintenance, crew training and emergency readiness, with performance monitored and corrective action taken.

Questions about safety?

Ask us about our safety practices, crew and compliance documentation before you book. We are happy to talk it through with you.

Working with your team

If you travel with security personnel, we work closely with them. Speak to us before your flight about any special requirements for an official or delegate.

Confidentiality

Departure, arrival and destination details are kept confidential as far as possible.

Timeframes & clearance

Safety comes with responsibility. We plan clearance times for checks before, during and after each flight, and work with your officials in the event of a security concern.

Firearms & weapons

If your security personnel need to carry firearms, tell us well before the flight so that it can be arranged in line with the applicable regulations. Firearms are otherwise not permitted in luggage.
