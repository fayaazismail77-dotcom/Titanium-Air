About us

Big enough to get the job done, small enough to care.

Titanium Air is a private charter company based at Lanseria International Airport. Colin Gilmour founded it in 2014, and it has grown with one idea: flying should feel like the start of the trip.

We fly small, medium and large groups, from light aircraft that land on bush airstrips to jets for business and leisure. You choose the day, the time, the airport or airstrip and the destination, and we arrange the rest around you.

Quality, safety and responsibility

Your safety comes first.

Safety comes first on every Titanium Air flight. We plan each trip with care, from choosing the right aircraft and crew to checking the weather, the airstrip and the paperwork before you fly.

  • Careful planning. Aircraft, crew, route and airport or airstrip are matched to your trip and confirmed in your written quotation.
  • Clear rules on baggage. We explain what you can and cannot bring, so there are no surprises at the airport. See the FAQ below.
  • Questions about approvals or insurance? Ask us before you book and we will answer them for your flight.

We work closely with your security personnel and keep your safety as our first responsibility.

  • Special requests. If an official or delegate has particular requirements, speak to us before the flight so they are arranged in advance.

  • Clearance times. We plan for safety checks before, during and after every flight, and work with your officials if a concern arises.
  • Firearms and weapons. If your security team must carry firearms, tell us before the flight so the paperwork is in order.

We want to run Titanium Air in a way that is good for our guests, partners and the communities around us.

  • Community. We aim to support local initiatives where we operate.

FAQ

Before you fly.

What is the baggage allowance?

Shuttle passengers may bring one checked bag and one small carry-on item. Our aircraft are smaller than airliners, so space is limited.

  • Checked bag: up to 20 kg, including camera or filming gear. Maximum size 40 cm wide × 30 cm high × 60 cm long.
  • Carry-on: up to 5 kg.
  • Soft bags or duffels with one flat side are best. Soft bags are on sale in our lounge.
  • Oversized items cannot be carried on your lap.

Limits can vary by aircraft and are subject to change, so check your quotation.

What if I have excess baggage?

Anything over 20 kg must be pre-booked. Unregistered excess baggage may not be loaded. Extra weight can carry a surcharge, and very heavy loads may need a freight seat at extra cost. Everything depends on the aircraft's weight and balance.

Return travellers can use our complimentary luggage storage at the Johannesburg and Kruger terminals. Stored luggage stays the passenger's responsibility.

Which items are not allowed?

Dangerous goods and security-restricted items cannot go in checked luggage, hand luggage or on your person. Common examples:

  • Flammables: lighter or heater fuel, paints, solvents, thinners, matches, bleaches and fibreglass repair kits.
  • Firearms and explosives: firearms of any type, ammunition, fireworks, flares and smoke canisters.
  • Infectious or biological material: cultures, samples and the equipment that handles them.
  • Corrosives: mercury, acids, alkalis and wet-cell batteries.
  • Compressed gases: oxygen cylinders, butane and propane, scuba tanks and non-refillable gas cartridges.

At check-in you confirm you carry no dangerous goods. Ask us for the full, current list.

What are the benefits of flying privately?

You save time because there are no airport queues. You set the departure time and date, relax in the VIP lounge beforehand, and sit anywhere in the cabin except the cockpit, with far more room than a commercial flight.

Can I bring my security team?

Yes. Talk to us before the flight about security staff, delegates or officials and any firearms they must carry, so everything is cleared in advance.

Can you fly to my destination?

We arrange flights across South Africa and to international destinations. The map above shows some of the routes we fly from Lanseria. If your destination is not listed, send us an enquiry and we will tell you what is possible.
